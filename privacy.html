Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 03, 2026

1. Who we are

Solar Prototype Inc ("we", "us") operates the SuryaDash Dashboard (the "Service"), a web application for monitoring solar thermal collector systems.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: the email address and password (hashed by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your raw password) you use to sign in.
  • Sensor data: temperature and flow readings from your SuryaDash hardware, which you own and control. This data belongs to you.
  • Local browser storage: your dashboard preferences (theme, units, alarm thresholds, poll interval) are stored in your browser's localStorage — this never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.
  • Usage analytics (only if you have enabled analytics — see Section 6): anonymized page views and interaction events, if a tracking ID has been configured.

3. How we use your data

  • To authenticate you and show you your own site(s) and sensor data
  • To send you alerts if you've configured browser push notifications
  • To improve the Service based on aggregated, anonymized usage patterns (if analytics is enabled)

We do not sell your data. We do not share your sensor readings with third parties.

4. Where your data is stored

Account credentials and sensor data are stored in Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Authentication), hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. Firebase's own privacy and security practices apply to the underlying infrastructure: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Data retention & deletion

Your sensor history is retained for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at test@example.com. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.

6. Cookies & local storage

We use localStorage (not traditional cookies) to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. If you've enabled analytics (Section 7), a small number of first- or third-party cookies may be set by the analytics provider. See the cookie banner on first visit for details.

7. Analytics

If enabled, we use no analytics provider (analytics disabled) to understand aggregate usage. This is optional and only activates after you accept the cookie banner. You can opt out at any time by declining or clearing the consent choice in your browser.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data (e.g., under GDPR or CCPA). Contact test@example.com to exercise these rights.

9. Security

We use Firebase's authentication and database security rules to restrict access to your data to your own account. No system is perfectly secure — if you discover a vulnerability, please report it to test@example.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
